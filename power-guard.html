The Energy Crisis: Factories in Gazipur currently operate at 30% capacity due to LNG shortages. Reliance on captive diesel generation pushes energy costs to $0.32/kWh (vs grid $0.12). Outages cost >$800/hr in lost production.

Peak Shaving: Using the PZEM-004T split-core sensor, we monitor power at 1Hz. This enables automated "Peak Shaving"—shedding non-critical loads (AC, Fans) 180 seconds before a spike trips the generator, saving roughly $180/month per line.

The Analytics Pipeline

  • Vectorization

    TS2Vec + MOMEMTO. Traditional forecasting fails due to "concept drift" (machines aging changing their power signature). Our memory gate patch allows the model to retain a baseline over months of operation without manual retraining.

  • Change Point Detection

    Bayesian CPD (Ruptures). Identifies structural changes in consumption patterns (e.g., a motor seizing up) to trigger preventative maintenance.
