Fig 1: Smart Bangladesh 2041 Industrial Corridor (Gazipur - Narayanganj - Chattogram)

The 20% Tariff Floor: With US duties stabilizing at 20% (down from the threatened 37%), the comparative advantage of cheap labor has effectively eroded against near-shoring competitors like Mexico. To survive, Bangladeshi factories must generate margin internally through rigorous efficiency.

The "Retrofit Innovation" Strategy: Traditional "Industry 4.0" requires replacing machinery ($12,000+ CAPEX). We reject this. We utilize a "Brownfield" approach—deploying commodity edge compute nodes* and recycled sensors to inject intelligence into existing legacy infrastructure.

*Edge Compute Node (e.g., Raspberry Pi 5): A credit-card sized industrial computer capable of processing AI models locally, ensuring data sovereignty and zero latency.
